Privacy Policy

App name: Taiwan Toilet Finder (台灣找廁所)
Effective date: October 10, 2026

Taiwan Toilet Finder (the “Service”) is a free tool that helps you find the nearest public toilets in Taiwan. We care about your privacy. This page explains how the Service handles data.

1. Data we do not collect

  • The Service has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address or any other personal information.
  • The Service has no ads and does not use any advertising or behavioral analytics tools.

2. Location data

  • The Service only reads your location after you tap the “Find the nearest toilet” button and grant location permission in your browser or device.
  • Your location is used only on your device to calculate distances and sort results. It is not uploaded to or stored on any server of ours (the Service has no server of its own) and is not shared with anyone.
  • You can turn off location permission at any time in your device or browser settings. You can still search by place name or address.

3. Content stored on your device

So the app can work when your signal is weak, the Service stores its program files and the Taiwan toilet dataset on your device (browser cache). This content is public data and contains no personal information. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data removes it. Your language choice (Chinese or English) is also saved on your device.

4. Third-party services

When you use the Service, the following third parties receive ordinary connection information (such as your IP address) in order to provide their services. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers: receive the map area you are viewing when map images load.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare): provides the Leaflet map library.
  • Google Maps: opens only when you tap “Navigate”, with the destination coordinates; Google then handles data under its own policy.

5. Data sources and disclaimer

  • Toilet data comes from the Ministry of Environment (Taiwan) “National public toilet registry”, used under the Open Government Data License. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Data is registered by various agencies and may contain location errors, or toilets that are closed or unavailable. Please check conditions on site. The Service is not for emergencies; in an emergency call 119 or 110 (Taiwan).
  • Place names and addresses are shown in Chinese, as provided by the official data.

6. Children

The Service does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
